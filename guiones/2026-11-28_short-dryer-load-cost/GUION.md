  In the federal test, a standard dryer dries an 8.45 lb load (10 CFR 430, appendix D2). A new standard vented electric dryer must dry at least 3.73 lb per kWh (10 CFR 430.32), so that load takes at most about 2.27 kWh: about 41¢ at the US average electricity price (18.19¢/kWh, EIA, Jan–Aug 2026), 33¢ in Washington and 44¢ in Illinois. The test assumes 283 loads a year, about 5.4 a week.
