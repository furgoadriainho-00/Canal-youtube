# How we work · Home By The Numbers

**Channel:** [@HomeByTheNumbers](https://www.youtube.com/@HomeByTheNumbers) — what the systems and appliances in a home really cost to run, explained with official data. English, for a US audience.

## Who does what
| | Script writer (Claude) | You (video editor) |
|---|---|---|
| Topic & research | Picks topics people search for and checks every number against official sources (EIA, U.S. Department of Energy, ENERGY STAR) | — |
| Script | Writes the narration, visuals, on-screen text, thumbnails and YouTube metadata | Reads it and flags anything unclear |
| Voice | — | Records the narration in US English, exactly as written |
| Video | Provides 3D clips, logo, banner and watermark | Edits, animates and adds music |
| YouTube | Provides title, description, tags, chapters and settings | Uploads, sets the thumbnail and publishes on the scheduled day |

## Where everything is
- **Scripts:** https://github.com/furgoadriainho-00/Canal-youtube/tree/master/guiones
  - One folder per video, named `YYYY-MM-DD_<topic>` (the date is the publish day).
  - `GUION.md` is the script, `fuentes.md` the sources, `datos/` the math.
- **Read first:** `guiones/EDITOR_GUIDE.md` (style, rules and assets).
- **3D clips:** `recursos/clips3d/` (15 appliances, 3 s each).
- **Brand files:** `canales/how-your-home-works/marca/` (logo, banner, watermark).

## Schedule
- **1 Short every day** (30–60 s) and **2 long videos a week**, on Monday and Thursday (8–12 min).
- New scripts are added to GitHub every night, always **at least one week ahead**.

## The 5 rules
1. **Don't change numbers** or sentences with data. If something doesn't work, tell us first.
2. **No copyrighted material.** Only Pexels or Pixabay stock, your own assets or ours, and music from the YouTube Audio Library.
3. **Keep it visual:** change the picture every 2–4 s, use animated numbers, and almost no static text.
4. **Audio:** around −14 LUFS, with music 18–20 dB below the voice.
5. **After publishing**, send us the video link so we can log it and track the results.
