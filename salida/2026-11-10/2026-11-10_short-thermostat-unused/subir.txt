TÍTULO:
65M Homes Have This Thermostat Feature. 20M Use It #shorts

DESCRIPCIÓN:
About 65 million US homes have a programmable or smart thermostat, but only about 20 million let it adjust the temperature automatically (EIA Residential Energy Consumption Survey 2020). ENERGY STAR certified smart thermostats must cut heating run time by at least 8% and cooling by 10%, roughly $100/yr for a home with gas heat and central air.
