TÍTULO:
Which Appliances Use the Most Electricity? Ranked by Cost

DESCRIPCIÓN:
Which appliances use the most electricity in your home, and what does each one really cost per year? I ranked 15 household appliances using the U.S. government's own Residential Energy Consumption Survey and 2026 electricity prices.

The top three cost about $500 a year each. A forgotten second fridge costs almost as much as your main one. And the #4 spot surprises most people.

How the numbers work: average yearly kWh per household that has each appliance (EIA RECS 2020) × the average U.S. residential price in 2026 (18.19¢/kWh, EIA, Jan–Aug 2026, preliminary). Your own costs will vary with climate, rates and usage.
