TÍTULO:
Heat Pump Water Heater Savings by State #shorts

DESCRIPCIÓN:
A heat pump water heater (ENERGY STAR, UEF ≥ 3.30) uses about 1,951 fewer kWh a year than a standard 50-gallon electric tank (UEF 0.92). What that's worth depends on your electricity price: about $281/yr in Washington, $299 in Florida, $313 in Texas, $375 in Illinois, $571 in New York and $636 in California. Sources: ENERGY STAR, 10 CFR 430.32(d), EIA RECS 2020, EIA electricity prices by state (Jan–Aug 2026).
