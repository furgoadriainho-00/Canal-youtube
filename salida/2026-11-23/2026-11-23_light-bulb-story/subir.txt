TÍTULO:
The Light Bulb Story: From Edison to LEDs

DESCRIPCIÓN:
The first practical light bulb lasted 13.5 hours in 1879. Today an LED gives the same 800 lumens as a 60-watt incandescent for about 9 watts. Here's the story in six moments, and what one bulb costs to run at each step (800 lumens, 3 hours a day, 18.19¢/kWh US average): 60 W $11.95/yr · 43 W (2014 limit) $8.56 · 17.8 W (2022 limit, 45 lumens per watt) $3.54 · 9 W LED $1.79 · about 6.4 W (2028 limit) $1.28.
