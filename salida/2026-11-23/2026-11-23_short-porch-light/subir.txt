TÍTULO:
Your Porch Light Costs More Than You Think #shorts

DESCRIPCIÓN:
A 60-watt incandescent porch light on 12 hours a night costs about $47.80 a year at the US average electricity price (18.19¢/kWh, EIA, Jan–Aug 2026). A 9-watt LED with the same 800 lumens (FTC): about $7.17. About $40.63 saved from one bulb. In 2020, about 13.62 million US homes left at least one incandescent or halogen bulb on outdoors all night (EIA RECS 2020, table HC5.1).
