Your Dehumidifier Costs More Than Your Fridge #shorts

DESCRIPCIÓN:
A dehumidifier costs the average home that has one about $182 a year to run, over 60% more than the main refrigerator ($112). Data: EIA Residential Energy Consumption Survey 2020 × 2026 U.S. electricity prices.
