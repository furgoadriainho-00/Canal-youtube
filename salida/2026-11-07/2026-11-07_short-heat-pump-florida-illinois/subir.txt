TÍTULO:
Heat Pump: Saves in Florida, Costs More in Illinois #shorts

DESCRIPCIÓN:
Same heat pump, opposite result: in Florida it heats a typical home for about $340 a year vs $431 with a new gas furnace; in Illinois it costs about $1,246 vs $556. The difference is mostly the price of gas. Data: EIA RECS 2020, EIA 2026 electricity and 2025 gas prices, ENERGY STAR and 10 CFR 430.32.
