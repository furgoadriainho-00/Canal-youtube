TÍTULO:
Do Heat Pumps Stop Working Below Freezing? #shorts

DESCRIPCIÓN:
Myth: heat pumps stop working below 32°F. Cold air still holds heat: measured from absolute zero (−459.67°F), 0°F air has about 87% of the heat energy of 70°F air. ENERGY STAR cold-climate heat pumps must deliver at least 1.75 units of heat per unit of electricity at 5°F; an electric baseboard heater delivers 1.
ENERGY STAR criteria: https://www.energystar.gov/products/air_source_heat_pumps/key-product-criteria
