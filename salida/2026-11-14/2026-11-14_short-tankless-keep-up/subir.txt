TÍTULO:
Can a Tankless Water Heater Keep Up? Quick Test #shorts

DESCRIPCIÓN:
A tankless water heater heats water only as fast as it flows. ENERGY STAR lists typical flow rates of about 2.5 gallons per minute for a shower, 2.2 for a kitchen sink and 3.3 for a clothes washer. A shower plus a sink is 4.7 GPM, while an ENERGY STAR gas tankless unit only has to deliver at least 2.8 GPM at a 67°F rise. Add up what runs at the same time before you buy. Sources: ENERGY STAR tankless gas water heaters and water heater criteria.
