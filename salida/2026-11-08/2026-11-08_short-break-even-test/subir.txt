Will a Heat Pump Save YOU Money? 30-Second Test #shorts

DESCRIPCIÓN:
Will a heat pump save you money? Divide your electricity price ($/kWh) by your gas price ($/therm) and multiply by 27.8. That's the efficiency a heat pump needs to beat a new 95% gas furnace. A minimum-efficiency heat pump is about 2.2. Sources: 10 CFR 430.32(c)(5), ENERGY STAR furnace criteria.
