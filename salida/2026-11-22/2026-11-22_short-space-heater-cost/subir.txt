TÍTULO:
What a Space Heater Costs Per Hour, by State #shorts

DESCRIPCIÓN:
A 1,500-watt space heater at full power uses 1.5 kWh per hour. Cost per hour at average residential electricity prices (EIA, Jan–Aug 2026): Washington 22¢, Florida 23¢, Texas 24¢, Minnesota 25¢, US average 27¢, New York 44¢, Massachusetts 45¢, California 49¢. Eight hours a day for 30 days: about $52 in Washington and $117 in California. Your cost: 1.5 × your rate per kWh. Heaters with a thermostat cycle off, so real use is often lower.
EIA electricity prices by state: https://www.eia.gov/electricity/monthly/epm_table_grapher.php?t=epmt_5_6_b
