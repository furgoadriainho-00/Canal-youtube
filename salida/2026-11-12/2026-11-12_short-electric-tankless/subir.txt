TÍTULO:
Electric Tankless Water Heater: Read This First #shorts

DESCRIPCIÓN:
Electric tankless water heaters are rated about 0.91 (UEF), basically the same as a standard 50-gallon electric tank at 0.92, so the yearly cost barely changes (~$492 vs ~$498). An ENERGY STAR heat pump water heater (UEF ≥ 3.30) costs about $137 a year. Sources: 10 CFR 430.32(d), ENERGY STAR, EIA RECS 2020.
