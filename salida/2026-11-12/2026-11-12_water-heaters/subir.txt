TÍTULO:
Tank vs Tankless vs Heat Pump Water Heater: Real Costs

DESCRIPCIÓN:
Tank vs tankless vs heat pump water heater: which one actually saves money? Using the official efficiency ratings (federal minimums and ENERGY STAR) and the government's data on real hot-water use, I compared yearly running costs for an electric home and a gas home.

Electric home: tank about $492/yr, electric tankless about $498, heat pump water heater about $137 (saves ~$355/yr).
Gas home: minimum gas tank about $269/yr, ENERGY STAR tankless about $159, heat pump water heater about $165.

How the numbers work: hot-water energy from the EIA Residential Energy Consumption Survey 2020; efficiencies (UEF) from 10 CFR 430.32(d) for a 50-gallon medium-draw tank and from ENERGY STAR criteria; EIA 2026 electricity and 2025 natural gas prices. Running costs only, not purchase or installation.
