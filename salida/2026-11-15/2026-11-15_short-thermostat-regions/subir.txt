TÍTULO:
Smart Thermostat Savings by Region #shorts

DESCRIPCIÓN:
At the ENERGY STAR minimum (8% less heating and 10% less cooling run time), a home with gas heat and central air saves about $108/yr in the Midwest, $111 in the South, $93 in the Northeast and $80 in the West. In the Midwest most of it comes from heating ($74); in the South, from air conditioning ($71). Data: EIA Residential Energy Consumption Survey 2020, EIA prices, ENERGY STAR connected thermostat criteria.
