TÍTULO:
How Much Does an EV Add to Your Electric Bill? #shorts

DESCRIPCIÓN:
Driving the average 11,026 miles a year (FHWA) at 30 kWh/100 mi (typical EPA rating for 2025 EVs) adds about 3,308 kWh a year, about 31% of an average home's 10,791 kWh (EIA). That's about 276 kWh or $50 a month at the U.S. average electricity price (EIA, 2026), vs about $105 a month for gas at 27.2 mpg (EPA) and $3.10/gal (EIA, 2025).
