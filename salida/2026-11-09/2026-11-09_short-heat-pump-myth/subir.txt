TÍTULO:
Heat Pumps Always Lower Your Bill? Myth or Reality #shorts

DESCRIPCIÓN:
Myth or reality: a heat pump always lowers your heating bill? Myth. It's about 2.2× as efficient as electric heat, but at average U.S. prices electricity costs ~3.6× as much as gas for the same energy. In Florida a heat pump saves about $90/yr; in Minnesota it costs about $520 more. Data: EIA, ENERGY STAR, 10 CFR 430.32.
