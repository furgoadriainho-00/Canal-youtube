TÍTULO:
Does a Smart Thermostat Actually Save Money? The Math

DESCRIPCIÓN:
Does a smart thermostat actually save money? ENERGY STAR certified models have to prove at least 8% less heating run time and 10% less cooling run time using real data from hundreds of homes. For a typical home, that works out to roughly $50–$100 a year.

But the government's household energy survey shows the real problem: about 65 million US homes have a programmable or smart thermostat, and only about 20 million let it adjust the temperature automatically.

How the numbers work: heating and cooling use from the EIA Residential Energy Consumption Survey 2020, valued at EIA 2026 electricity and 2025 natural gas prices; savings assume run-time reductions translate into similar energy reductions. Your savings depend on your home, schedule and rates.
