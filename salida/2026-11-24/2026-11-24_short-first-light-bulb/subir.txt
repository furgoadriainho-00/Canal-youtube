TÍTULO:
The First Light Bulb Lasted 13.5 Hours #shorts

DESCRIPCIÓN:
October 22, 1879: Edison's lab tests a carbonized cotton-thread filament that glows for 13.5 hours (Thomas A. Edison Papers, Rutgers). 1882: Pearl Street Station starts supplying electricity, about 400 lamps on day one (IEEE). 2014: federal limit of 43 W for an 800-lumen bulb (10 CFR 430.32). Today: an 800-lumen LED uses about 9 W (FTC). At 3 hours a day and 18.19¢/kWh (EIA): $11.95 a year for a 60 W bulb vs $1.79 for the LED.
