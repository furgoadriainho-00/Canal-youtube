TÍTULO:
Is Charging an EV at Home Always Cheaper? #shorts

DESCRIPCIÓN:
On U.S. averages, fuel for a gas car costs about $1,255 a year vs about $602 to charge an EV at home. Washington saves about $1,198 a year; Massachusetts, with electricity near 30.2¢/kWh, only about $205, and about $15 in a worst case with charging losses added. Data: FHWA, EPA, EIA (gasoline 2025, electricity Jan–Aug 2026), ORNL.
